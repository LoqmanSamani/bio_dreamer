## Why This Matters for BioDreamer

BioDreamer is built on the principles of model-based RL. By learning a world model of biological environments, we can plan optimal interventions (mutations, perturbations) in imagination, dramatically reducing the need for expensive real-world experiments.

This post provides the theoretical foundation for the approach used throughout BioDreamer.
