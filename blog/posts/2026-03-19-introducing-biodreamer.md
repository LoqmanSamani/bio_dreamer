# Introducing BioDreamer: World Models for Biological Design

Biology is expensive to query at every scale. A single round of directed evolution costs months and thousands of dollars. A genome-wide CRISPR screen burns through millions of cells. Yet intelligent design demands exploring vast combinatorial spaces.

BioDreamer applies **JEPA-based world models** and **Active Inference** to plan mutations, molecular dynamics, and gene perturbations in imagination before touching a single pipette. The framework contains three modules (ProteinDreamer, MolDreamer, CellDreamer) that share a unified latent dynamics backbone built on the Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture.

This post covers the problem, the architecture, the three modules in detail, and where the project is headed. It draws on 22 papers spanning world models, Active Inference, protein fitness landscapes, generative molecular design, and gene regulatory dynamics.
